What you get

Jaguar CD, two ways

CUE/BIN, CDI and CHD boot via an HLE CD BIOS or the real BIOS on emulated BUTCH hardware. Audio CDs and the VLM work. CHD needs a session-aware chdman; each release attaches one.

Link play, self-configuring

JagLink and CatBox serial at register level. Automatic mode rides a RetroArch netplay session with nothing to type; direct TCP finds LAN hosts by UDP beacon and lists them by name. Doom deathmatch validated, and Ultra Vortek dials the emulated Voice Modem (press 911 at the title screen).

user guide · voice-modem.md

Jaguar GameDrive

16 MB bank switching from RetroHQ's GDBIOS bindings. GD-locked homebrew boots.

ROM patches

Softpatching for cartridges (IPS and friends, frontend-applied) works out of the box, and a curated known-patch catalogue documents target CRCs. CD images load by path and are not softpatched.

rom-patches.md

Peripherals

ST/Amiga mouse (all three wirings), the Tempest 2000 rotary, the TR10 analog and driving protocol, and the port-1 lightgun. Per-axis dead zone, offset and response tuning on every analog source. The Team Tap and the Pro Controller are not emulated yet.

epic #428 · v3.4.0 notes

HD texture pipeline

Dump every unique blit source as a hashed PNG, redraw it, drop it in a pack directory. Replacement is presentation-only; savestates and netplay stay bit-identical with or without a pack.

texture-dump.md · #369

Boot ROM selection

Series K or Model M (both embedded), or a custom image identified by checksum. Unrecognized 128 KB images still load, with a log warning.
