Beyond the headless gate help wanted

The matrices above prove that titles boot and execute. They cannot prove a game plays correctly to the end, and no amount of headless sweeping will change that.

Report gameplay issues in Discussions with your frontend log attached. The crash watchdog writes the failure signature into it.
