## Description

Virtual Jaguar brings Atari Jaguar and Jaguar CD emulation to your Mac.
Organize your game collection in a visual library, connect a controller, and
play in a desktop window or a controller-friendly TV interface.

Save states let you return to a moment in a game. Configure your controls,
use the on-screen Jaguar keypad, and customize the interface with themes and
fonts. Built-in debugging and inspection tools help you explore Jaguar software.

You supply your own game files. No commercial games or BIOS files are included.
Compatibility and performance vary by game and Mac; see the project website
for compatibility information and documentation.

Virtual Jaguar is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with
or endorsed by Atari. Atari and Jaguar names belong to their respective owners.
